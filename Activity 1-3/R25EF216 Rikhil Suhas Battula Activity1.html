Stadium Guidelines

1. Ticket Verification

E-tickets with valid QR codes must be displayed on mobile devices or physical printouts at the entry turnstiles.

2. ID Proof Requirement

All ticket holders must present a government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID) matching the ticket name.

3. Prohibited Items

Bags, power banks, coins, cameras, water bottles, and outside food items are strictly prohibited inside the stadium.
